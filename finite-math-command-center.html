Get the right calculator now

A TI-83 or TI-84 Plus is recommended. No phones, no watches, no CAS calculators (TI-89/N-Spire), and Desmos and Excel are banned on both exams. Buy/borrow it early so Camila practices on the exact device she'll test with.

Bank the easy 40%

  • Discussions / check-ins / scans (5%): no late discussions accepted — do them the week they open.
  • Late penalty: 4%/day unless arranged with the instructor before the due date.
